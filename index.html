Research directions

Questions worth solving.

I connect methodological machine-learning research with practical problems where transparency, fairness, and reliable decisions matter.

01 / AI AND SOCIETY↗

Responsible & fair AI

Understanding and mitigating bias in NLP and machine learning, including stereotype-aware and task-adaptive approaches.
02 / MODEL TRANSPARENCY↗

Explainable machine learning

Developing interpretable decision-support approaches for education, health, and environmental forecasting.
03 / COMPLEX SYSTEMS↗

Multi-agent systems & simulation

Modeling human behavior and operational systems using causal, agent-based, and surrogate modeling methods.
04 / REAL-WORLD APPLICATIONS↗

Forecasting & applied AI

Using learning models for energy demand, climate variability, transportation, and other complex prediction tasks.

TU Delft research software

AATOM: the underlying simulator.

AATOM — An Agent-based Airport Terminal Operations Model

The open-source AATOM simulator models airport handling processes and passenger activities. It was introduced by Stef Janssen and collaborators. The 2023 paper coauthored by Sahand Ziabari explicitly uses AATOM as its underlying detailed model for interpretable surrogate experiments.

Simulator repository ↗2019 AATOM simulator paper ↗2023 surrogate-modeling paper ↗2023 article PDF ↗

Attribution: the 2019 AATOM simulator paper is by Janssen, Sharpanskykh, Curran and Langendoen; it is presented as foundational software, not as a publication authored by Sahand Ziabari.

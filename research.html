Scholarship & publications

Research at the intersection of AI and complex systems.

I study how artificial intelligence can support trustworthy decisions and how computational models can help us understand complex real-world systems. My work spans fairness-aware machine learning, explainability, multi-agent modeling, forecasting, and applied computer vision.

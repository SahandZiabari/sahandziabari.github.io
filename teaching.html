Academic mentorship

Learning through real problems.

I have supervised bachelor's and master's theses across machine learning, NLP, explainable AI, forecasting, and interdisciplinary applied research. At the University of Amsterdam, this work included collaboration with industry and public-sector partners.

I also develop course materials and assessment structures, with a focus on connecting theory to problems students can investigate and evaluate. I hold the University Teaching Qualification (BKO) from the University of Amsterdam.

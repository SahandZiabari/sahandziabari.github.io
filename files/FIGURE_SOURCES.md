### Why some papers have no picture

For the student outcome RL–MIL paper and the airport AATOM surrogate modeling paper, the authentic figures were verified in the published PDFs, but no stable, licensed standalone image URL suitable for embedding was confirmed. The cards are deliberately text-only instead of using a figure from an unrelated source or inventing one.

**Note:** Images are loaded from external publisher servers; a publisher may later change or restrict hotlink access. If any is blocked, the figure source can still be opened via the source link. A future local-hosted edition should use the original high-resolution figure files supplied by the coauthors.
